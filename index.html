Questions people actually ask

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the page removes your preview.

Why is my photo still over the limit?

Very large or detailed photos need a smaller width. Try 800 px maximum width, or check whether your form allows a range rather than one exact number.

Will the photo look blurry at 20 KB?

Some loss is unavoidable at very small sizes. A tight crop before resizing helps far more than raising quality after the fact — start from the original, not a WhatsApp copy.

Important: every portal sets its own pixel and KB rules. This tool helps you meet the KB limit, but always read the official notification for your exam or form.
