How to Make a Passport Photo at Home

Stand against a plain white wall in daylight, face the camera directly, and keep a neutral expression. Take the photo from chest-up, then crop it with the Passport Photo Maker to 35 x 45 mm. For official applications, also check the authority's rules on background, glasses, head size, and recency — size alone is not enough.
