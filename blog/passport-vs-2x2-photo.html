35x45mm vs 2x2 Inch Photo: What Is the Difference?

A 35 x 45 mm photo is a rectangle, taller than wide. A 2 x 2 inch photo is square. They are not interchangeable. Use the Passport Photo Maker and pick the exact size your authority asks for.
