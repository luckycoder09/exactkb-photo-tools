How to Resize a Signature to 20KB for PAN and Exam Forms

Sign with black ink on plain white paper, photograph or scan it in bright, even light, and crop tightly so there is almost no empty space around the signature. Then use the Signature Resizer and pick 20KB. A tight crop is the single biggest factor in reaching a small KB size without making the signature unreadable.
