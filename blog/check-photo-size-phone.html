How to Check a Photo's Size in KB on Android and iPhone

On Android, open Files or Gallery, tap Details, and look for the size in KB or MB. On iPhone, open Photos, swipe up, and look for size and dimensions. If it is over your form's limit, resize it with the Photo Resizer and check the downloaded file again.
