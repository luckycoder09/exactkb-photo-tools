How to Resize a Photo to 20KB for SSC Forms — Step by Step

SSC forms commonly reject photos over the stated KB limit. Start with a clear, well-lit photo, resize it to 20KB with the Photo Resizer, download it, and check the file size on your device before uploading. If the face looks blurry at 20KB, try a tighter crop first — a smaller crop compresses better than a full-room photo.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading a screenshot instead of the original photo.
  • Using a PNG photo — JPEG compresses much better for photos.
  • Not checking the downloaded size — always verify, do not assume.
