How to Resize a Photo for an IBPS Form Without Rejection

Read the limit printed in your own application form, then use the Photo Resizer and pick that exact KB value. Crop so the face fills most of the frame before resizing: a tight crop reaches a small KB size with far less blur than a wide photo shrunk down.
