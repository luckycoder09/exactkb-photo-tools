JPEG vs PNG for Exam Photos: Which One Should You Upload?

For a real photograph, JPEG is almost always right: it compresses to 20KB or 50KB with acceptable quality, while PNG stays large. PNG is better for logos and line drawings. If your form accepts JPEG, upload JPEG.
